Prototyping - Fall 2026

Prototyping is commonly taught as the acquisition of tool-specific skills. This course takes a different starting point: prototyping is treated as an enactive practice through which creativity takes place, and the practice itself becomes an object of study. Across the course students prototype in different traditions and use each cycle to interrogate what counts as a prototype, what creative actions a prototype enables, and which competencies it surfaces.
