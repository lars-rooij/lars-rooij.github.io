Based on some physical experiments I did for different parts of this class! A lot more dynamic of shapes, and they were fun to try. I found out a better way to curve things in blender, and got more comfortable with dynamic shapes in Shapr3D. I especially like the flower in Shapr3D. It's a lot easier to make smooth things in Shapr3d, but I still miss the feeling of controle that I get from blender.

This week I focussed on basic shapes, mainly consisting of a curved material with a small diameter, or my bottle. I think most notable the 3D bottle gave me the most struggle in blender with combining the curve hard corners. It was fairly easy to make in shapr3D. On the other side, the half-up half-down metal wire was easier to make in blender, because I could more manipulate lines into being 3D. There was no concernible difference between the programs for the other object. The hardest to make was the rubber band, because I intended to include the square crossection it has, and the twists it makes. I settled on mimmicking the shape, and hope to retry this later.

Currently writing this in week 1, I intend to improve, include and practise with more details over time.
